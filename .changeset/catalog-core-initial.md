First release: the PostgreSQL catalog model as TypeScript types, and the pure functions over it.

- The model describes the tables, columns, constraints, indexes, row security policies, enums, and
  domains of the configured schemas as PostgreSQL built them. A catalog module generated by
  `@nizam-os/pg-catalog` is assignable to it, with every name and bound as a literal type.
- `toPublicCatalog(catalog, tables)` returns the slice a browser may see: the listed tables with
  their types, nullability, default literals, and constraint rules, and exactly the enums and
  domains they use. Expressions, policies, indexes, and comments are dropped; an unknown or
  duplicate table is a `TypeError` naming it.
- `diffCatalogs(expected, actual)` compares two catalogs member by member and returns the added,
  removed, and changed members with their paths, depth first.
- No dependencies; runs on Node, Bun, Deno, Cloudflare Workers, and in browsers.
